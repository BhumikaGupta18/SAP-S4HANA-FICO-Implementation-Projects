# Scenario 2: Business Partner (CVI) & Procure-to-Pay with APP

## Business Background
The procurement department is onboarding a strategic supplier and requires Business Partner integration, down payment processing, 3-way matching, and automatic vendor payment processing.

## Scope
- Business Partner (CVI)
- Vendor Creation
- Payment Terms
- Vendor Down Payment
- Purchase Order
- Goods Receipt
- Invoice Verification
- Automatic Payment Program (APP)

## Key SAP T-Codes
BP, F-48, F-54, MIGO, MIRO, FBZP, F110
